About Me

I’m Alexis — known online as ManifestingLex, lexingtongee, and DJ 6LA. At 31, I was diagnosed with breast cancer and am currently navigating a double mastectomy, chemotherapy, and reconstruction. Refusing to let it slow me down, I’m learning to DJ, chasing music festivals, and building the life I always manifested for myself. My content is raw, hopeful, and honest — real treatment updates sit alongside GRWMs, wellness, and DJ practice, showing what it actually looks like to keep living fully through cancer. I manifested everything I have up to this point, and I’m manifesting beating this too.
